## 1. Featured reviews

### Ferrari SF90 Stradale
*Rosso Corsa · 1,000 hp · 0–100 in 2.5 s · €4,900 / day*

**The prancing horse, electrified — and more savage than ever.**

Ferrari's first series-production plug-in hybrid rewrites what a supercar can
be. A twin-turbo V8 works in concert with three electric motors to unleash a
full 1,000 hp, launching you to 100 km/h in a scarcely believable 2.5 seconds
and on to 340 km/h. Yet the SF90 is not merely a missile: its torque-vectoring
chassis and razor-sharp steering make it feel impossibly agile at any speed.
Slide into the cockpit and every surface, every stitched panel, is a reminder
that this is Maranello's state of the art.

*Perfect for:* the guest who wants the fastest, most technically advanced Ferrari
ever built — and the unmistakable theatre of a V8 howl.

### Lamborghini Revuelto
*Verde Mantis · 1,015 hp · 0–100 in 2.5 s · €5,200 / day*

**The V12 rebellion continues — now with a lightning bolt.**

The Revuelto is Lamborghini's answer to the hybrid age, and it answers with a
naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12 joined by three electric motors for a combined
1,015 hp. The result is the most powerful Sant'Agata road car yet: a 350 km/h
statement sculpted from carbon and raw attitude. It is loud, theatrical and
deliberately outrageous — exactly as a flagship Lamborghini should be. This is
the car that turns a hotel forecourt into a stage.

*Perfect for:* guests who want maximum drama, maximum presence and the sound of
twelve naturally aspirated cylinders.

### Porsche 911 GT3
*Guards Red · 510 hp · 0–100 in 3.4 s · €1,800 / day*

**The purist's benchmark — motorsport DNA you can drive every day.**

Ask any engineer what a sports car should feel like and they will point to the
911 GT3. Its 4.0-litre naturally aspirated flat-six sings to 9,000 rpm, the
steering speaks in whispers and the balance is so transparent it flatters the
driver rather than intimidating them. With 510 hp and a chassis honed at
Flacht, the GT3 is fast, but its real magic is precision. It rewards skill and never
feels out of its depth on the road.

*Perfect for:* discerning drivers who value feel, balance and an honest,
analogue connection over outright numbers.

### Rolls-Royce Phantom
*English White · 571 hp · 0–100 in 5.3 s · €2,500 / day*

**The definition of arrival. Silence made magnificent.**

The Phantom is not a car you drive so much as a world you enter. Its 6.75-litre
twin-turbo V12 is so hushed you measure progress in wafts, not revs, and the
"Magic Carpet Ride" suspension erases the surface beneath you. Behind the
coach doors lies a sanctuary of hand-finished wood, deep lambswool and the
signature Starlight headliner — an illuminated night sky above your head.
Presence, comfort and imperious calm, in equal measure.

*Perfect for:* weddings, VIP transfers and any occasion where arriving is the
main event.

### Bentley Continental GT Speed
*Beluga Black · 650 hp · 0–100 in 3.6 s · €1,900 / day*

**Grand touring perfected — a grand piano wrapped in velvet.**

The Continental GT Speed pairs a muscular 6.0-litre W12 with a chassis that
hides its two-and-a-half-tonne kerb weight astonishingly well. All-wheel drive,
rear-wheel steering and active anti-roll bars make it carve corners far better
than any car this luxurious has a right to. Inside, Crewe's craftsmanship is
everywhere: knurled metal, diamond-quilted leather and a rotating dashboard
that turns from veneer to touchscreen at the touch of a button.

*Perfect for:* long-distance elegance with a genuine turn of pace when the road
opens up.

### Aston Martin DB12
*Satin Sepang Bronze · 680 hp · 0–100 in 3.6 s · €2,400 / day*

**A grand tourer with a sports car's heart — and a spy's charisma.**

Aston calls the DB12 the world's first "super tourer", and the claim holds. A
4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 delivers 680 hp with effortless authority, while a
newly bonded aluminium structure and recalibrated suspension sharpen responses
to a genuine sports-car level. The cabin is a lesson in restraint and quality —
driven by its own bespoke infotainment rather than a borrowed one. Beautiful,
willing and quietly ferocious.

*Perfect for:* guests who want understated British elegance with modern,
confident performance.

### McLaren 720S
*Volcano Yellow · 720 hp · 0–100 in 2.9 s · €3,200 / day*

**Carbon, clarity and a supercar that feels almost telepathic.**

The 720S is engineering as art. A carbon-fibre monocoque keeps mass low, the
twin-turbo V8 delivers 720 hp with a fierce, linear surge, and the glass-panel
cockpit gives the driver unrivalled visibility. Its supple-riding Proactive
Chassis Control means it is as usable on a long cruise as it is devastating on
a track. Fast, focused and beautifully engineered — the thinking driver's
supercar.

*Perfect for:* enthusiasts who appreciate lightweight precision and a driving
experience stripped of compromise.

### Mercedes-AMG G 63
*Manufaktur Green · 585 hp · 0–100 in 4.5 s · €1,100 / day*

**An icon that refuses to go quietly — or anywhere it can't.**

Four decades of essentially one shape, endlessly refined. The G 63 pairs its
rugged, unmistakable silhouette with a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 and the
side-exit exhaust note that announces it from a street away. Inside, however,
it is pure modern luxury: twin widescreen displays, quilted Nappa leather and
materials worthy of an S-Class. Commanding, characterful and effortlessly cool.

*Perfect for:* city glamour, mountain escapes and guests who want presence with
personality.

### Audi R8 V10 Performance
*Daytona Grey · 620 hp · 0–100 in 3.1 s · €1,700 / day*

**The everyday supercar — a naturally aspirated V10 farewell.**

The R8 is that rare thing: a mid-engined supercar you could genuinely use every
day. Its 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 delivers 620 hp with one of the
finest soundtracks in motoring, while quattro all-wheel drive turns any weather
into an advantage. Build quality is impeccable, visibility is superb and the
whole car feels engineered for confidence at speed. Civilised when you want it,
electrifying when you don't.

*Perfect for:* guests who want exotic performance without exotic temperament.

### Porsche Taycan Turbo S
*Frozen Blue · 761 hp · 0–100 in 2.8 s · €1,500 / day*

**Electric performance with zero compromise on soul.**

The Taycan Turbo S proves electrification loses nothing. Two motors deliver
761 hp through a two-speed rear gearbox, rocketing you to 100 km/h in 2.8
seconds with a launch-control surge that borders on violent. An 800-volt
architecture means rapid charging, and the low centre of gravity gives it
astounding body control. Silent, savage and exquisitely built — the future,
beautifully realised.

*Perfect for:* guests wanting cutting-edge EV performance with genuine Porsche
dynamics.

### BMW M5 CS
*Frozen Deep Green · 635 hp · 0–100 in 3.0 s · €1,000 / day*

**The sharpest, lightest, most focused M5 ever built.**

The CS is BMW M at its most ruthless. Weight is trimmed, power is up to 635 hp,
and the chassis is honed into something that feels alive beneath you. Carbon
seats, gold accents and a stripped-back intensity set it apart from the standard
M5, yet it keeps the four doors and everyday usability that make the formula so
compelling. A limited-run super saloon — understated to look at, devastating to
drive.

*Perfect for:* guests who want near-supercar pace with four seats and a sense of
fastidious engineering.

### Maserati MC20
*Bianco Audace · 630 hp · 0–100 in 2.9 s · €2,400 / day*

**The trident's return to the front line.**

The MC20 marks Maserati's rebirth as a true supercar maker. Its bespoke
"Nettuno" 3.0-litre twin-turbo V6 — a Formula 1-derived, 630 hp masterpiece —
sits in a carbon monocoque, giving the car a purity and agility that shout
engineering ambition. Butterfly doors open to a cabin that is driver-focused and
beautifully restrained. Rare, ravishing and unmistakably Italian.

*Perfect for:* guests who prize exclusivity and a supercar that few others on
the road can claim.
